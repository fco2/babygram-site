Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

BabyGram ("we", "our", or "us") is a baby activity tracking app for parents and caregivers. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect location data, payment card details, photos, contacts, or browsing history.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and sync baby activity logs across your devices
  • To enable shared access with co-caretakers you invite
  • To manage your account and subscription
  • To send activity notifications (only if you enable them)
  • To export your data as a CSV file on your request
  • To diagnose and fix app crashes — using platform crash reports from Google Play and Apple as our baseline, plus more detailed diagnostics from Firebase Crashlytics only if you opt in (see section 8)

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sell it to any third party.

3. Third-Party Services

BabyGram uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, cloud database, and push notifications. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — subscription management. RevenueCat receives your user ID and purchase history. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — optional, opt-in crash diagnostics on Android. Disabled unless you turn it on in the app. See section 8. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing — payment processing. Payment card details are handled entirely by Google and never seen by us. Google Payments Privacy Notice

4. Data Sharing

Your data is never sold. It is only shared in these cases:

  • Co-caretakers you invite — baby profiles and activity logs are visible to users you explicitly invite to share a baby profile.
  • Service providers — Firebase and RevenueCat process data on our behalf as described above.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect the safety of users.

5. Children's Privacy

BabyGram is designed for use by parents and caregivers (adults). The app is not directed at children under 13. While the app records data about infants and young children (activity logs, profiles), all accounts are created and managed by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data — including baby profiles and activity logs — is permanently and immediately deleted from our servers.

7. Your Rights

  • Access & export — you can export all your activity data as a CSV file from the Settings screen in the app.
  • Correction — you can edit your display name and baby profile details at any time within the app.
  • Deletion — you can delete your account and all data instantly from the app (Profile → Delete Account), or by submitting a deletion request.

8. Optional Crash Diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics)

To keep the app stable, we rely first on the crash reports that Google Play and Apple provide to developers as standard. Those platform reports are our baseline crash-reporting mechanism and do not depend on any choice you make in the app.

On Android, BabyGram also includes Firebase Crashlytics, which gives us more detailed diagnostics for users who choose to share them:

  • It is optional and opt-in. Crashlytics collection stays disabled unless you affirmatively turn it on under Settings → Privacy → "Detailed crash diagnostics". It is not pre-selected, and the app is fully usable if you leave it off.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time from that same setting. Turning it off stops further collection. It is as easy to withdraw as it is to grant.
  • We do not intentionally attach personal content. We do not attach your name, email address, account or user ID, baby information, activity-log entries, notes, or any other user-provided data to Crashlytics reports — not through user identifiers, custom keys, breadcrumbs, logs, or manually reported errors.
  • Crashlytics may still process some technical data during a crash and at ingestion: the crash stack trace, device and operating-system information, a random Firebase installation identifier generated by Google (not linked by us to your account), and your IP address as part of delivering the report.
  • These identifiers can still be personal data. A random installation identifier and an IP address may qualify as personal data under the GDPR even when they are not connected to your name or account.
  • Crashlytics is Android only. On iOS, BabyGram does not include Crashlytics; only Apple's platform diagnostics apply.

Recipient. Crashlytics data is processed by Google (Firebase Crashlytics), acting as our service provider / data processor. It is not shared with co-caretakers, advertisers, or data brokers, and it is not sold.

International transfers. Google may process this data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google makes available its Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms, which incorporate the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, and Google self-certifies under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. We rely on those Google-provided safeguards; this policy describes the mechanisms Google offers and does not represent that we have independently verified them.

Retention. Crashlytics data is retained by Google under Firebase Crashlytics' own retention schedule (individual crash events for a limited period, currently around 90 days). We do not keep a separate copy.

9. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in Firebase Firestore with access rules that restrict each user to their own data. Authentication is handled by Firebase Auth, supporting Sign in with Google and Sign in with Apple.
